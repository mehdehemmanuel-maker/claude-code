# Research: Civil engineering data and taxonomies: what may be used, and the names index

One research pass, 2026-10-31, done by a research agent reading public sources (product pages, documentation, papers, benchmarks). These are its findings in short, kept so they are not lost; a line names its source where the research gave one (the full reports, with their links, were returned in the session of 2026-10-31). Where a figure came from a vendor, or from an aggregator rather than the paper itself, the line says so; such figures are claims, not measurements of ours. Nothing here was measured in Nexus unless it says so.

- Legal: designations (C30/37, #5, IPE 300, A-2-4, GW) are fine as names; copying standards' tables is not. CC BY-ND: ship unmodified file + separate mapping.
- Usable: ulmewix/Steel_Section_Database (MIT; EN IPE/HEA/HEB/HEM/IPN/UPN/UPE/SHS/RHS/CHS; nulls not invented; small, cross-check vs timskovjacobsen/steelprofiles_api MIT and ArcelorMittal Excel). AISC shapes: NOT redistributable (user imports own copy locally). Rebar/concrete/timber: names yes, figures derived (pi d^2/4) or public (USDA Wood Handbook PD). Soil USCS/AASHTO symbols yes (USBR Earth Manual public). FHWA: 10 controlling criteria memo (fhwa.dot.gov/design/standards/160505.pdf), HDS-5 culverts (public). OSM taginfo keys/values (ODbL, attribute). IFC 4.3 ADD2 = ISO 16739-1:2024, CC BY-ND: IfcRoad/IfcRailway/IfcBridge/IfcMarineFacility; 645 psets. web-ifc MPL-2.0 browser reader. bSDD API (synonyms, per-dictionary licence). Uniclass 2015 CC BY-ND, 12 tables, from NBS. ETIM: ODC-BY, free -> best embeddable product taxonomy. Wikidata CC0 synonyms/translations. NO: ECLASS, MasterFormat; UNCLEAR: OmniClass, UNSPSC, CoClass (steward changing after 2026-06-30).
- Import first (nodes): EN sections (ulmewix, cross-checked), rebar & concrete classes (derived), soils, FHWA culverts/road criteria, USDA timber, web-ifc reader to explode real IFC bridges/roads.
- Names first: IFC entities+PredefinedType spine; Uniclass Co/En/EF/Ss/Pr; OSM infra tags; designation vocabularies; ETIM; Wikidata aliases; bSDD on demand.
- Schema NameEntry {id, term, synonyms[{text,lang,kind alt|abbrev|hidden|trade|plural}], category_path (disciplines.ts ids), facets, routes_to[{node_id,weight,reason}] may be empty, broader/narrower/related (SKOS), equivalents[{scheme,code}], source{scheme,code,version,url,licence,retrieved}, counts_as_node:false (gate)}. Routing: exact/alias -> routes_to; empty -> walk broader -> "nearest real node". McMaster lesson: route to family then facets.
- Open: ArcelorMittal terms; ND derivative question; road values only via FHWA/state DOT quotes; loads no open source -> names + user inputs.
