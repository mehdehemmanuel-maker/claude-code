# Research: AI and "vibe" CAD tools

One research pass, 2026-10-31, done by a research agent reading public sources (product pages, documentation, papers, benchmarks). These are its findings in short, kept so they are not lost; a line names its source where the research gave one (the full reports, with their links, were returned in the session of 2026-10-31). Where a figure came from a vendor, or from an aggregator rather than the paper itself, the line says so; such figures are claims, not measurements of ours. Nothing here was measured in Nexus unless it says so.

- Headlines: (1) field settled on LLM writes editable source, real kernel runs it (Zoo KCL, VibeCAD VibeScript/FreeCAD, CADAM OpenSCAD, VibeSCAD OpenSCAD+BOSL2, CAD-Recode CadQuery, CAD-Assistant FreeCAD py). (2) numbers beat pictures: BenchCAD (no renders = same score at ~half cost; models write silhouette-overlap code & hill-climb), ModelRift (renders caught nothing that mattered; volume/angle/interference/strain did), Spectral Labs correct only within 0.1% volume/COM/distance. (3) assemblies fail on placement: BenchCAD 2.0 83-96% of zero-score part types right shape wrong place. (4) accepted revision vs failed candidate (VibeCAD); Zoo AGENTS.md + agent notes, selection awareness, mass/COM/volume tools.
- implicit.build: browser lattice designer; PicoGK (LEAP 71) compiled to wasm + in-house implicit-step STEP import; 32 threads, crossOriginIsolated, 4GiB; TPMS (gyroid, Schwarz P, diamond, Neovius, Fischer-Koch, I-WP, F-RD), beam (BCC FCC octet octahedron), Voronoi; stress-graded; topopt; thermal/flow demos; lattice library E*=C rho^n "starting point, not a test"; no LLM; no B-rep out; v0.0.0, no team named.
