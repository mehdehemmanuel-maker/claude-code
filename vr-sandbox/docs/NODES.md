## Evidence

What each node's own contribution rests on, read from what it is (src/nexus/evidence.ts); a value on a wire is as strong as the weakest node it rests on.

| Evidence | Nodes | Means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| exact | 84 | holds exactly: a definition, geometry, counting |
| measured | 1 | read from a measurement (a die traced, a test run) |
| specified | 4 | a standard's, a maker's or a reference table's figure |
| derived | 336 | worked out from what it is given by laws that hold here; no weaker than its inputs |
| given | 13 | set by whoever made the board |
| empirical | 47 | a fit to measurements, or a material's own behaviour: holds over the range it was fitted to |
| estimated | 6 | typical values or an estimate, said so |
| simplified | 39 | a model that leaves things out (a perception model, a rigid-body simulation, an approximation) |
| hypothetical | 430 | a design or a guess: what something would be, not a measurement of a thing that is |

Checked: 10 compared with an independent reference (laws the rest of the book reaches another way, agreeing); 904 tested here (their own tests: that they do what they say, not that the world does); 46 untested; none validated against experiment.

Built before the update of 2026-10-28 (laws with their units, evidence and ±, materials down to their elements): 123 of 960 (dated in src/nexus/built.ts from git): their tests may be flawed, and each is to be re-checked against it; marked *before the update* below.
