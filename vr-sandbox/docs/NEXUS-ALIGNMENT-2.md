# Nexus: alignment check 2

The question asked: does the manifold represent reality as one continuously connected space of possible states,
transformations, scales and timescales, or as a collection of objects with properties attached to them? And does it
represent what every element and material is made of, how it behaves and changes in every environment, and what
happens during the change, down through the subatomic and up through the planetary?

The answer is no. Scale, time and observation are now generated, but matter is not. What is missing is one
foundational representation, named at the end.

So the manifold is connected in its carriers (one mechanism generates the laws of every conserved quantity), in its
scales and times (one generator for every matter's mechanisms) and in its observers (one projection for every sense).
It is still a catalogue in what matter is. A material is a named list of numbers, and the levels above and below it
are not connected to it by any transformation the language holds.

## What is missing

**Matter as constituents bound by interactions, at every level, with each level's quantities coarse-grained from the
one below.** This is the same move that replaced flows with carriers in round 0.

1. **Identities as carriers.** What a transformation conserves is counted: each element's atoms, charge, and below them
   nucleons and leptons. A reaction, a decomposition or an ionization is then a balance of those carriers, so the
   machinery that already generates every carrier's laws generates which transformations are possible. A matter is a
   composition: amounts of constituents, not a name.
2. **Binding as interaction energy and length.** Constituents are held by interactions, each with an energy, a length and
   a stiffness. The stated continuum quantities become coarse-grainings of them. Density comes from mass over the
   volume a constituent takes, stiffness from binding energy over its length cubed, and sound speed, heat capacity and
   conductivity follow from those. Each coarse-graining keeps its transformation, so the level below can be generated
   again where a phenomenon needs it.
3. **State as a position in an energy landscape, and change as motion across it.** Which arrangement a composition takes
   follows from binding energy competing with thermal energy (kT), mechanical energy (pressure times volume, stress
   times strain) and field energy. Phase, bonding and ionization are which minimum is occupied. "During the change" is
   the crossing between minima, at a rate set by the barrier against kT (Arrhenius, Eyring) and by the time each
   mechanism takes. This is the energy counterpart of what round 6 did for times: there a regime is a ratio of times,
   here a state is a ratio of energies.
4. **Local clocks.** Each region of a realization advances at the time its own mechanisms need, the times scale.ts
   already generates, with its interfaces bound by how fast information crosses between regions. The tuner's single
   axis becomes a field of resolutions over the manifold: refine where the phenomenon needs it, coarse-grain where it
   does not, and keep the transformation either way.

## Why this is the primitive and not more data

Stating more materials, or the 118 elements' properties, would grow the catalogue, not the language. The test of the
primitive is compression. The 33 materials' stated densities, stiffnesses and sound speeds should be reproduced from
fewer quantities: their compositions and the interactions of their constituents. A residual whose structure names what
the interactions lack is the failure that grows the language.

## What it would not yet reach

Biology, organisms and ecosystems need the same move one level up: structures whose constituents are themselves
compositions, held by interactions that are flows of energy and matter rather than bonds. The subatomic needs quantum
states, which the language does not express: a state there is an amplitude over possibilities, not a position in a
landscape. Both are named here so they are not mistaken for done when the material level is.

## Since this check

Round 8 began the missing layer (docs/NEXUS-PROBES.md). Identities are counted at a level of energy, transformations
are balances, a molecule's enthalpy comes from its atoms and bonds, and a phase is the least Gibbs energy. Boiling
points, vapour pressures and a decay's missing particle follow from it. A solid's continuum quantities, rates across
barriers and local clocks remain.
