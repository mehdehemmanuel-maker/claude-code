# One owner per concern

The long form of CLAUDE.md's owners index: what each owner is responsible for, in full. Read the row before touching
a concern, and extend its owner rather than writing a second one. CLAUDE.md keeps the short index; this keeps the
detail, because a row that is a paragraph makes the anchor too long to read before a round, which is the drift it
exists to prevent.
