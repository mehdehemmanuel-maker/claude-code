# Lessons that check what you do

"Lessons that check what you do" ranked among the first things to build, over every want asked of Ego. A lesson here is not a script (`src/assistant/lesson.ts`). It is any design Ego can make, built first on her bench, then turned into the steps of building it yourself. Each step shows what to do and is done only when it is done in your world.

| Step | Done when |
|---|---|
| place | a part of that kind and material, about that size, where the guide shows it: each face of its box within 6 cm of the guide's, so it is turned as it should be too |
| join | those two parts are joined, by the joint the step names or another of the same model (rigid for rigid, a hinge for a hinge) |
| test | the build is played, and every joint still holds after three seconds under its own weight; a joint that breaks sends you back to fix it |

The steps follow the bench's own order, which is the order the design is put together in. Whatever you build that the lesson didn't ask for is yours: it neither counts nor gets in the way.

## Checked

- A table becomes its parts to place, its joints to make and a test.
- In an empty world nothing is done, and the guide shows the first part.
- A part half a metre off its guide doesn't count.
- Built as the design says, every place and join step is done. The test waits for Play, and is done only after three seconds of it.

## Ask her

- *"teach me to build a table"*
- *"show me how to build a shelf that holds 20 kg"*
