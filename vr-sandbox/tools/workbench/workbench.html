How an arrangement carries a load

Bars carry a load by stretching only if the load lies in the span of what they resist. Otherwise the members must bend. The count is linear algebra; the stiffness comes from a frame of members that bend.

Wood as a test of the counting

Seven kept woods: stiffness along the grain and across it against the solid's share (wood's density over the cell wall's, 1500 kg/m³). Measured values from the USDA Wood Handbook; nothing is fitted into the counting. Along the grain the walls run with the load; across it they must bend.

A stream changed through its surface

A round stream heated from its surface reaches the threshold at its centre after its own time, while it moves. The length it must be held over is the Fourier number times flow, density and heat capacity, over π times conductivity. Its diameter does not enter.

What it takes

Defaults: PLA, the kept intent's values. The roughly 10 mm³/s of an ordinary hotend is a commonly reported figure, an estimate.

Erase

Each bit erased sends at least k T ln 2 of heat to where its heat goes: the second law.

Hold

A bit held for a time sits behind a barrier crossed less than once in that time: at least k T ln(t k T / h), the same law that sets how fast molecules change.

Hear

Nothing told travels faster than light, so a lag bounds how far apart the parts of what acts as one can be.

Where Nexus is

A generator of structure from wants on regions, with laws generated from conserved carriers and checked against kept human knowledge as evidence. Each round found a missing distinction and made it general.
